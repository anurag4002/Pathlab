# 🧪 Pure Path Lab

> 🏥 A modern and professional Laboratory Management System for managing patients, diagnostic cases, billing, reports, and laboratory operations from a single platform.

Pure Path Lab is a full-stack pathology and diagnostic laboratory management application designed to simplify day-to-day lab operations while providing a clean and intuitive experience for administrators, laboratory staff, and patients.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 👨‍⚕️ Patient Management
- 🧾 Billing & Invoice Management
- 🧪 Laboratory Case Management
- 📄 Diagnostic Report Management
- 💰 Transactions & Ledger Management
- 👥 Referral Doctor Management
- 📊 Business & Case Reports
- 🔍 Global Search
- 📱 Patient Report Portal
- 🔐 Secure Admin Authentication
- 📲 Patient access through Mobile Number + OTP
- 📱 Fully Responsive Interface
- 🗄️ MongoDB Database
- ⚡ REST API based architecture

---

## 🖥️ Modules

### 👨‍⚕️ Patient Management

Manage complete patient records including:

- Patient registration
- Personal information
- Contact details
- Case history
- Referral information
- Patient-wise records

---

### 🧾 Billing & Cases

Manage laboratory billing and diagnostic cases efficiently.

- Create new bills
- Generate invoice references
- Track payment status
- Manage pending/partial/paid bills
- View case-wise billing information
- Search invoices and patient records

---

### 🧪 Laboratory Management

Organize laboratory-related operations from a centralized dashboard.

- Lab tests
- Diagnostic cases
- Report management
- Test information
- Case tracking

---

### 📄 Reports

Manage diagnostic reports securely.

- Create and manage reports
- View patient reports
- Upload/report files
- Patient-wise report access
- Report status management

---
